Step 04

Pre-inspection cheat sheet

Housing-industry items to check before moving forward.

Property review checklist

Use this as a discussion aid—not as a substitute for a licensed inspection, legal advice, or local requirements.

Questions to raise

  • Are permits and renovation records available?
  • What is the age and service history of major systems?
  • Are there known water intrusion or drainage issues?
  • What does the inspection contingency and deadline say?
  • Are insurance, flood, wildfire, or other location risks relevant?
  • Are HOA rules, assessments, or restrictions disclosed?
Inspection scope, disclosures, insurance availability, and contract deadlines vary by location. Verify with qualified local professionals.

Real Estate Brokerage Agreement — Sample

Client: [Client legal name]

Brokerage: [Licensed brokerage legal name]

Scope: The brokerage will provide agreed real estate representation services for the property search and transaction described in the signed agreement.

Term: [Start date] through [end date / termination terms]

Compensation: [Clearly state agreed amount or method, when due, and any applicable conditions. All compensation is negotiable.]

Agency and disclosures: Explain representation, duties, conflicts, and required state/local disclosures.

Signatures: Client [signature/date] · Brokerage representative [signature/date]

Illustrative outline only. Not a legal form and not for signature. Use a current brokerage-approved, jurisdiction-specific agreement reviewed by qualified counsel.

Before sending: confirm correct parties, representation type, term, compensation language, required disclosures, and brokerage approval.

House-hacking ideas to explore

A house hack may mean living in one unit of a duplex/triplex and renting another, sharing a spare room, or checking whether a separate suite/ADU is legal to rent.

Rule #1: Buy a home that still works for your budget if rental income is lower than expected or the unit sits vacant. Verify zoning, permits, HOA rules, insurance, lease requirements and lender rules before counting on rent.
